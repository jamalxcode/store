# Kuwait Single-Person Company Website Compliance Audit

**Review date: 18 November 2026.** Use this checklist for a Kuwait-registered Single-Person Company (شركة الشخص الواحد), whether the site is informational, takes orders directly, or is used alongside a marketplace. Record what the site actually does: a catalog or social account that accepts orders by WhatsApp or direct message is not just an informational site for audit purposes. The legal form is a company under Article 85, not automatically a sole proprietorship (مؤسسة فردية); verify the form on the register extract before using this checklist ([Kuwait Companies Law No. 1/2016, Arts. 4 and 85](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/ar/laws/kw-kuwait-companies-law-law-1-2016)).

## Start here: identify the rules that apply

| Audit branch | Apply when | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| **Baseline: all registered companies** | The business has a website or another electronic channel | Arabic trade name and commercial register (CR) number on the electronic channel; valid licence and correct activities; required details on company documents ([Commercial Register Law No. 18/2018, Arts. 3, 6, 8–9](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf); [Companies Law, Art. 22](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/ar/laws/kw-kuwait-companies-law-law-1-2016); [Commercial Licensing Law No. 111/2013, Art. 1](https://kbc.moci.gov.kw/Files/Decisionsandlaws/4dc32f72-3253-41fb-a0ee-567ff62314ac.docx)). |
| **Consumer-facing** | You offer, advertise, sell or supply goods or services to consumers, including online | Product and price information, Arabic advertising and invoices, applicable returns, warranties and approvals ([Consumer Protection Law No. 39/2014](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [Decision No. 27/2015, executive regulations](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf)). |
| **Freelance / no commercial premises** | You hold a licence under the special regime for scheduled activities, not merely because there is one owner | Apply Decisions 216/2026 and 217/2026; verify the specific schedule entry, additional approvals and transition status ([MOCI Decision 216/2026](https://files.moci.gov.kw/mocisite/assets/content/uploads/2026/11/ccccab68-82f6-4fae-8a7d-d575538d7804.pdf); [MOCI Decision 217/2026](https://files.moci.gov.kw/mocisite/assets/content/uploads/2026/11/c59b07ca-df4a-4256-88f9-64d85d3afe2f.pdf)). |
| **Intermediary platform or merchant on one** | You operate a covered ordering/delivery intermediary or sell through one as a merchant | Audit the operator and merchant duties separately. Direct-only stores that list no third-party products or services are excluded from this decision ([Decision 109/2026, Arts. 1–2, 8–9](https://alitqanlg.com/delivery-platforms-regulation/)). |
| **Digital Commerce Decree-Law** | You sell, supply, display or advertise products or services digitally | Prepare now; do not mark these obligations enforceable solely because the decree was published. Article 45 starts the law one month after its executive regulations appear in the Official Gazette. As of this review I could not verify that publication or an effective date; have MOCI or Kuwait counsel confirm before sign-off ([Decree-Law No. 10/2026, Arts. 1, 44–45](https://alitqanlg.com/decree-law-no-10-of-2026-digital-commerce/); [MOCI decisions register](https://moci.gov.kw/MociSite/decisions/index.html); [reported court reasoning on commencement](https://www.aljarida.com/article/145723)). |

**Status key:** Required = verified rule for the stated scope. Conditional = check the actual activity or licence before marking Pass or N/A. Recommended = sensible control, not a verified universal legal requirement. Readiness = implement or plan for the new digital-commerce regime, then reclassify after verifying commencement.

## Company identity and online placement

| ID | Status | Pass test and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ID-01 | Required | Obtain the current MOCI licence, commercial register extract and any sector approvals. Match the Arabic trade name, legal form, CR number, licensed activity codes, expiry dates and registered details; verify any service or product offered by the site is within scope. Keep copies in the audit file ([Commercial Licensing Law, Arts. 1–2, 6 and 11](https://kbc.moci.gov.kw/Files/Decisionsandlaws/4dc32f72-3253-41fb-a0ee-567ff62314ac.docx); [Commercial Register Law, Arts. 5–6](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf); [ONE MOCI](https://www.moci.gov.kw/MociSite/en/e-services/index.html)). |
| ID-02 | Required | Display the registered trade name together with its **commercial register number in Arabic** on the website and the company's electronic channels. Search for the actual name and number on public pages and check for omissions, typos, or use of another entity’s number ([Commercial Register Law, Arts. 8–9](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf)). |
| ID-03 | Conditional / recommended | Treat the **commercial licence number (رقم الترخيص التجاري)** and **CR number (رقم السجل التجاري)** as separate fields. If the licence, an activity-specific decision or a regulator requires display of the licence number, follow the exact placement, wording or badge requirement. Otherwise, displaying it alongside the CR is recommended; the general rule I verified requires the Arabic trade name and **CR number**, not a separate licence number in a specified homepage position ([Commercial Register Law, Art. 8](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf); [Commercial Licensing Law, Arts. 1 and 11](https://kbc.moci.gov.kw/Files/Decisionsandlaws/4dc32f72-3253-41fb-a0ee-567ff62314ac.docx)). |
| ID-04 | Recommended placement | Put the identity block in the public site-wide footer in readable text, including on mobile and checkout. Repeat it on Legal/Contact and marketplace profiles you control. Check deep links, Arabic and English views, and pages seen without signing in; do not rely on a logo, an image of a licence, a hidden accordion, or terms alone. |
| ID-05 | Required on company and consumer documents | Include company name, legal form, CR number, capital and amount paid up on company-issued correspondence, receipts and other papers; say “under liquidation” where applicable. Put identifying registration details and any trademark on documents and correspondence with consumers, including electronic documents. Test quotes, confirmations, invoices, receipts and refund documents, not just the footer ([Companies Law, Art. 22](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/ar/laws/kw-kuwait-companies-law-law-1-2016); [Consumer Protection Law, Art. 19](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf)). |
| ID-06 | Required internally; recommended on site unless scope requires otherwise | Keep the registered Kuwait domicile and required registry details up to date; file notifiable register changes within 30 days. On the Contact page, provide a monitored business email and telephone and an appropriate business, correspondence or returns address where applicable. Do not publish a private home address as a premises open to customers ([Companies Law, Art. 23](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/ar/laws/kw-kuwait-companies-law-law-1-2016); [Commercial Register Law, Art. 6](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf); [MOCI Decision 216/2026, Arts. 3 and 10](https://files.moci.gov.kw/mocisite/assets/content/uploads/2026/11/ccccab68-82f6-4fae-8a7d-d575538d7804.pdf)). |

Suggested footer copy (use the exact registered name; the last line is conditional or voluntary):

> [الاسم التجاري المسجل] | [الشكل القانوني المسجل]  
> رقم السجل التجاري: [رقم القيد]  
> رقم الترخيص التجاري: [رقم الترخيص، عند الاقتضاء]

## Sales, consumer rights and data

| ID | Status | Pass test and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CX-01 | Required for consumer offers | Check meaningful Arabic product or service information, clear prices, accurate characteristics, applicable warnings and warranty terms, and no misleading descriptions. Verify billing equals advertised pricing, and show the full order total, delivery fees, delivery timing, cancellation terms and payment choices before commitment as an audit control ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 12–13, 22 and 26](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [executive regulations, Arts. 28–30](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf)). |
| CX-02 | Required for consumer transactions | Place a test order and verify an invoice in at least Arabic. Check supplier name, address and telephone, invoice/transaction date, delivery timing, local-currency price, quantity and units, nature and essential characteristics, origin as applicable, supplier or representative signature or stamp, and applicable used-condition, serial and warranty details. Include the corporate and registration particulars in ID-05 ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 15 and 19](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [executive regulations, Art. 41](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf)). |
| CX-03 | Required rights; prominent web link recommended for the baseline | Write and test a returns/exchanges policy reflecting the statutory 14-day rule for eligible goods in their original condition; Art. 10 says from receipt, while the general regulation and MOCI retail guidance refer to purchase. For delivered orders, do not shorten the statutory receipt-based window by counting from payment. Test eligible discounted goods, refunds without fees or forced store credit, defective or nonconforming goods separately, and documented category exceptions (including qualifying 24-hour cases when the return option is on the invoice). Do not use a blanket “no refunds or exchanges” clause. The rules also prescribe a prominent Arabic returns notice for shop/store owners; a clearly visible web policy is a prudent digital equivalent, not a verified requirement to link to a government page ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 10 and 14](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [executive regulations, Arts. 25–27](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf); [MOCI notice on 24-hour exceptions](https://www.moci.gov.kw/MociSite/news/162/index.html)). |
| CX-04 | Recommended generally; conditional platform duties | Link to **Returns & Exchanges**, **Contact / Complaints**, and **Privacy** from the footer; surface the returns policy before checkout. Provide a working complaint email, phone or form, a reference number and a documented escalation path. For external escalation, link to the [Kuwait Government Online consumer complaints service](https://e.gov.kw/sites/kgoenglish/Pages/eServices/MOCI/ConsumerProtectionServices.aspx) and show [MOCI’s complaint number 135](https://moci.gov.kw/en/e-services/consumer-protection-and-commercial-control-sector/). I did not verify a universal rule requiring a direct government hyperlink. Covered delivery platforms have specific complaints and customer-service duties ([Decision 109/2026, Arts. 11–12](https://alitqanlg.com/delivery-platforms-regulation/)). |
| CX-05 | Required / conditional | Check applicable advertising permissions and obtain any required MOCI permit before promoting discounts or special offers; retain approvals, approved dates and price evidence. Verify Arabic advertising and remove false claims, false scarcity and misleading original prices ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 13 and 25–26](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [executive regulations, Arts. 31 onward](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf)). |
| CX-06 | Required data handling; conditional notice duties | Inventory data collected by forms, checkout, analytics and vendors; check legitimate purpose, permissions, confidentiality, safeguards, and access/correction processes. Publish a notice that reflects actual collection, uses, sharing, retention and contact routes; check CITRA rules if a covered provider and platform-specific notice duties if applicable. Do not assume the telecom/IT privacy regulation applies identically to every ordinary merchant ([Electronic Transactions Law No. 20/2014, Arts. 32–35](https://www.e.gov.kw/sites/KGOenglish/Forms/MagazineA.pdf); [CITRA Decision 26/2024](https://www.citra.gov.kw/sites/ar/Pages/DecisionsDetails.aspx?id=6); [Decision 109/2026, Art. 3](https://alitqanlg.com/delivery-platforms-regulation/)). |
| CX-07 | Conditional | Verify separate rules before enabling instalments, age-restricted products, regulated health/food/financial products, or other licensed services. Assign an owner for defect escalation, recall notices, warranty claims and after-sales support; where you are the manufacturer or local agent, check applicable publication of recall notices on the company or local agent’s website if it exists ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 16–18 and 21](https://pp.moj.gov.kw/ar/LawsPP/ConsumerProtectionLaw.pdf); [executive regulations, Arts. 42–43](https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/kuw184478.pdf); [Commercial Licensing Law, Arts. 1 and 9](https://kbc.moci.gov.kw/Files/Decisionsandlaws/4dc32f72-3253-41fb-a0ee-567ff62314ac.docx)). |

## Additional tests by business model

- **Freelance licence (Decisions 216/2026 and 217/2026):** Show the company name and CR number clearly on its website, if any, and on social accounts. Include the CR on official accounts and transactions; match the schedule description; check the company bank account and approved electronic payments; do not invite customers to a private residence; check annual reporting of applicable platform and social profile links. For products described in Article 11, check the name and CR on the product, package, invoice or electronic listing as appropriate. Retain evidence of transition compliance, where relevant ([MOCI Decision 216/2026, Arts. 6–11 and 14–16](https://files.moci.gov.kw/mocisite/assets/content/uploads/2026/11/ccccab68-82f6-4fae-8a7d-d575538d7804.pdf); [MOCI Decision 217/2026](https://files.moci.gov.kw/mocisite/assets/content/uploads/2026/11/c59b07ca-df4a-4256-88f9-64d85d3afe2f.pdf)).
- **Intermediary platform operator (Decision 109/2026):** Verify the correct activity code (532013), clearly disclosed identity and contact methods, terms and privacy policy, supplier details, pre-order prices and permitted fees, paid ranking labels, order correction, Arabic e-invoices, records, accessible human support, and published cancellation/refund and complaint procedures. Test the 48-hour complaint response, applicable seven-working-day compensation deadline and complaint retention period of at least six months from closure ([Decision 109/2026, issuing Art. 2 and regulation Arts. 3, 5 and 10–12](https://alitqanlg.com/delivery-platforms-regulation/)).
- **Merchant on a covered platform:** Verify licensing, accuracy of listings and offers, product conformity, price parity with the physical point of sale where applicable, no undisclosed fees, consent to post-order changes through the approved channel, and complaint cooperation. Do not treat platform-operator duties as if they all applied to the merchant’s independent website ([Decision 109/2026, Arts. 2 and 8–9](https://alitqanlg.com/delivery-platforms-regulation/)).
- **Decree-Law 10/2026 readiness:** Prepare for digital registration; disclose name, register and registration number, email, telephone and electronic contact links; show contracting steps, final all-in price, payment, delivery, offer validity and returns terms. Prepare immediate Arabic electronic invoicing, delay notifications, order-error handling, product returns, complaint records of at least six months and transaction records of at least five years. Verify cybersecurity, electronic signature, approved payment provider, advertising and influencer requirements when applicable. Recheck the executive regulations for additional fields, exceptions and retained record requirements before moving these tests into the mandatory column ([Decree-Law 10/2026, Arts. 3, 8, 10–28 and 44–45](https://alitqanlg.com/decree-law-no-10-of-2026-digital-commerce/)).

## Non-compliance and escalation

| Trigger | Verified consequence or limitation |
|---|---|
| **Omitting the required Arabic trade name and CR disclosure** | Commercial Register Law Art. 19 states a **KD 1,000–5,000** fine for breaches of Art. 8, among other listed provisions. This is not a fine for merely omitting a separate commercial licence number or for choosing a footer over a header ([Arabic Commercial Register Law, Arts. 8 and 19](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf)). |
| **Wrong or borrowed CR, or deliberately false registry filings** | Art. 19 provides a **KD 1,000–20,000** fine for Art. 9 violations or intentionally incorrect filings covered by the article. Art. 19 also provides for possible administrative closure and review by the competent court within ten days of the closure decision ([Arabic Commercial Register Law, Arts. 9 and 19](https://www.kuwaitchamber.org.kw/echamber/docfiles/website/CMS/trlawc1_2018B.pdf)). |
| **Supplier and advertising breaches** | Consumer Protection Law Art. 27 covers Arts. 12–19 (including pricing and promotions, defective-goods remedies, invoices and supplier identity in consumer documents): **up to KD 20,000 and/or two years’ imprisonment**, with doubling for qualifying recidivism and without prejudice to consumer compensation. Art. 30 separately provides **up to KD 5,000 and/or six months’ imprisonment** for advertiser violations of Arts. 25–26, subject to the statutory exception. Art. 31 provides for confiscation or destruction and possible closure for two to three months upon conviction; Art. 34 provides for corrective orders and specified safety measures ([Consumer Protection Law, Arts. 27, 30–31 and 34](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/laws/kw-kuwait-consumer-protection-law-law-39-2014)). |
| **Deficient company documents** | Art. 22 can make the legal representative jointly liable with the company for damage to a good-faith third party caused by the omission if company assets cannot cover the damage ([Companies Law, Art. 22](https://kuwaitlaw.ai/ar/laws/kw-kuwait-companies-law-law-1-2016)). |
| **Commercial licence conditions or fraud** | Licence cancellation and, in the specified cases, administrative closure may apply under Arts. 11 and 13. Art. 12 provides **KD 1,000–5,000 and/or up to one year’s imprisonment** for the specific Art. 11(5) and (7) triggers, not for every licensing or website defect ([Commercial Licensing Law, Arts. 11–13](https://kbc.moci.gov.kw/Files/Decisionsandlaws/4dc32f72-3253-41fb-a0ee-567ff62314ac.docx)). |
| **Intermediary platform sanctions** | A first-instance administrative court reportedly annulled Art. 15 of Decision 109/2026 in the Talabat case. Do not rely on the article’s warning, closure, revocation or blocking schedule as settled law; check current case status. The ruling did not invalidate the rest of the regulation ([reported judgment](https://www.aljarida.com/article/145723); [Kuwait Times on the ruling and appealability](https://kuwaittimes.com/article/50539/kuwait/other-news/court-strikes-down-delivery-penalties-raising-fears-of-weak-enforcement/)). |
| **Once Decree-Law 10/2026 is in force** | Art. 39 provides **KD 1,000–10,000 and/or up to one year’s imprisonment** for listed offences such as unregistered digital commerce, with doubling on recidivism. Art. 10’s store-disclosure duty is **not** among the provisions enumerated in Art. 39. Check separate administrative pathways under Arts. 20 and 34–37, and possible confiscation or closure under Art. 40 for qualifying offences ([Decree-Law 10/2026, Arts. 20 and 34–45](https://alitqanlg.com/decree-law-no-10-of-2026-digital-commerce/)). |

## Evidence and sign-off

Set each item to **Pass / Fail / N/A / Pending confirmation**. Record: `Control ID | URL or channel | evidence and test date | finding | priority | owner | fix-by date | retest`. Capture desktop and mobile screenshots, official licence and register extracts, a sample Arabic invoice, a sample order and refund, a complaint test, and copies of relevant approvals. Record the reason for every N/A.

- **Fix before go-live or immediately if live:** Invalid or mismatched licence, wrong or missing CR, unlicensed activities, unlawful terms, undisclosed fees, or no Arabic invoice on consumer orders.
- **Confirm before giving a clean sign-off:** Licence-specific display wording/placement, applicability of Decisions 216/217 or 109, sector requirements, and any new Gazette entry setting the digital-commerce effective date. Log the exact article, decision number and effective date once verified.
- **Retest after changes:** Recheck after redesigns, checkout migrations, adding a sales channel, changing the register/industry activity, licence renewal or new MOCI rules. Keep this checklist with the company’s compliance records; it is an audit tool, not a company-specific legal opinion.
