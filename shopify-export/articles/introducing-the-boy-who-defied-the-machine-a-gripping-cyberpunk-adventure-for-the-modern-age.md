# Introducing  "The Boy Who Defied the Machine" - A Gripping Cyberpunk Adventure for the Modern Age"

_2025-03-20T17:36:02+03:00_ · https://sala.company/blogs/news/introducing-the-boy-who-defied-the-machine-a-gripping-cyberpunk-adventure-for-the-modern-age

We are proud to announce the release of the new fiction book "The Boy Who Defied the Machine".

"The Boy Who Defied the Machine" by Jamal Faisal Almutawa offers readers a fast-paced cyberpunk thriller that feels eerily relevant in our age of rapidly advancing AI technologies. 

This novel follows ZK, a protagonist who transitions from a arrogant  hacker to a disciplined digital warrior. The world-building is detailed, with Al-Asima providing a rich setting that blends ancient architecture with futuristic technology.The book explores meaningful themes including:

- Balance between analog and digital worlds

- Dangers of over-relying on technology

- The value of unpredictable human creativity against systematic threats

.This book would appeal to fans of works like "Neuromancer" or "Ready Player One" who appreciate a more grounded approach to digital threats.
