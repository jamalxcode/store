1. Nature of Products

SALA CO sells exclusively digital products (eBooks, fonts, and educational software). All products are delivered electronically via download link immediately upon successful payment. Because digital content is consumed upon delivery, different rules apply compared to physical goods.

2. No Returns on Digital Content

In accordance with Kuwait's Consumer Protection Law No. 39/2014 and the Digital Commerce Law (2025), digital products that have been downloaded or accessed are exempt from the standard 14-day return/exchange right that applies to physical goods. Once a digital product has been delivered and the download link has been accessed, no return or exchange is accepted.

3. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund in the following circumstances only:

- The file is corrupt, damaged, or unreadable and we are unable to provide a functioning replacement within 72 hours.

- The product was not delivered to you due to a technical error on our part, and we cannot resolve it within 72 hours.

- The product description was materially inaccurate and the delivered content differs significantly from what was advertised.

Refunds will be processed via the original payment method within 7–14 business days of approval.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be granted in the following situations:

- You changed your mind after purchasing.

- You purchased the wrong product by mistake.

- You have already downloaded or accessed the product.

- Technical incompatibility with your personal device (please verify system requirements before purchase).

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email basketservicesllc@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with your order details.

6. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Kuwait. Any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of Kuwait.

7. Right to Cancel Due to Technical Error in the Contracting Process

In accordance with Kuwait's Consumer Protection Decree-Law and applicable e-commerce regulations, consumers have the right to cancel a transaction where a technical error occurred during the electronic contracting process — for example, where an error in the website, payment gateway, or ordering system caused an unintended or duplicate transaction.

How to raise a cancellation claim under this clause:

- Contact us by email at basketservicesllc@gmail.com with the subject line: "Technical Error Cancellation Request".

- Include your order number, the date of the transaction, a description of the technical error experienced, and any supporting evidence (e.g. screenshots, duplicate charge receipts).

- Claims must be submitted within 7 days of the date the transaction occurred.

Resolution timeframe: We will acknowledge your claim within 2 business days and aim to resolve it within 7 business days of receiving all required information. If a cancellation is confirmed, a full refund will be issued via the original payment method within 7–14 business days.

This right applies regardless of whether the digital product has been accessed or downloaded, provided the error is verifiable and the claim is submitted within the stated timeframe.
