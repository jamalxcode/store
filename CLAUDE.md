# CLAUDE.md

Rebuilding the Shopify ebook store at https://sala.company as a self-hosted site in this repo.

Read before working:
- `docs/SHOPIFY-AUDIT.md`: how the current store is built.
- `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`: must-haves. Kuwait Ministry of Commerce details (trade name, license 502625, address) on
  every page; Tap payments, confirmed server-side only; checkout asks for a verified email and a name, never a postal address.
- `docs/NEED-FROM-SHOPIFY.md`: open items waiting on the owner.

Facts:
- All products are digital. Prices are in KWD with 3 decimals.
- Policies are bilingual EN/AR. Carry them over word for word from `shopify-export/policies/`.
- Keep the Shopify URL paths (`/products/<handle>`, `/collections/<handle>`, `/blogs/news/<handle>`, `/policies/<name>`).
- `shopify-export/` is generated by `python3 tools/snapshot.py` (public data only). Don't hand-edit it; regenerate.
- Never commit secrets (Tap keys) or customer and order data.
- The house style of the other sala.company sites: static GitHub Pages site, custom domain via `CNAME`, `.nojekyll`,
  the shared GoatCounter snippet (`https://salacompany.goatcounter.com/count`), WCAG AA in light and dark mode, and no sideways scroll on phones.
